Share and back up

  1. Each hour: type P1 and P2 into that hour's row and press Enter. It saves straight away.
  2. Every day is recorded in the day-by-day record as soon as it has a figure. Nothing else to press.
  3. To show someone a day, open it and copy the share link. Whoever opens the link sees the same numbers.
  4. Once a week, download a backup. Figures live in this browser only, so the backup is what lets you restore them on another device or after clearing the browser.
